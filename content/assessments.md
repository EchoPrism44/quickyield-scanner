## Methodology

Assessments use Litmus's published A–F methodology with four defined signals:

1. **Liquidity** – pool depth and depth stability
2. **APY Stability** – yield variance over time
3. **Reward Quality** – token emission sustainability
4. **Data Completeness** – data source reliability and coverage

Each signal is scored 0–100, weighted, and combined into a single A–F letter grade with a weakest-factor summary.
