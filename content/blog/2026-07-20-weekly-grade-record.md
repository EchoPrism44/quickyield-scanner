## Our read

The pattern in this week's downgrades is unmistakable: almost every hard fall (B → F) was a **high-APY meme-token pair** — WSOL-ZEREBRO at 88% APY, WSOL-DADDY at 34%, ETH-TRAC at 30%. Headline yields like these come almost entirely from short-lived incentives and volatile trading fees, and when the volatility shows up in the data, the grade drops fast. That is the grade doing its job: **high APY never hides high risk for long.**

On the other side, the D and F buckets shrank (−20 and −24 pools) while B grew by 44 — the market's middle got a little safer this week, even as the degen end churned.

**What to watch next week:** whether the newly-upgraded B pools hold their grade — a pool that bounces F → B → F is telling you something the APY number won't.
